Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Homework Tracker desktop application (“Homework Tracker,” “we,” or “the app”) handles information when you use it. Homework Tracker is a student productivity project.

Information the app handles

  • Account information: If you create an account, the app sends your email address, display name, and password to Firebase Authentication for account creation, sign-in, email verification, and password recovery. Password handling is managed by Firebase; the app does not need to store your password in its local assignment file.
  • Assignments and settings: Assignments you create and app settings are saved in a local data file on the device where you run the app.
  • Remembered email: If the app's remember-email feature is used, the email address is saved locally on that device.
  • Google Classroom information: If you choose to connect Google Classroom, Google sign-in asks you to authorize access. The app retrieves your active courses and coursework, including assignment information, to display/import into the app. The connection is optional.

How information is used

Information is used to provide account access, display and organize assignments, support password recovery, and—only when you choose to connect it—retrieve Google Classroom course and coursework information.

Where information is stored and shared

Local assignments, app settings, and a remembered email are stored on your device. Account authentication is handled by Firebase. Google Classroom information is requested from Google after you authorize the connection. The app's use of Google APIs is also subject to Google's privacy practices and terms. We do not operate a separate public cloud database for your assignment list as described in this version of the app.

Information is transmitted to Firebase or Google when needed for the features described above. Network providers and those services may process technical information as part of providing their services.

Your choices

  • You can choose not to connect Google Classroom and can use manual assignment entry.
  • You can remove local app data from your device, bearing in mind this may permanently delete locally saved assignments and settings.
  • You can manage or revoke the app's Google account access through your Google Account security settings.
  • You can request account password recovery through the app's password-reset flow.

Children and students

Homework Tracker is designed as a student productivity tool. Users should follow their school’s technology rules and obtain any required parent or guardian permission before creating accounts or connecting school accounts.

Security

We use third-party authentication and Google's authorization flow for the features described. No app or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Do not share your password or OAuth authorization with anyone.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when changes are made. Do not rely on this policy if the app's actual behavior has materially changed without updating this page.
